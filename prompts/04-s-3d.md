## Tasks

1. **Derailleur.** "The derailleur must be more detailed, be attached to a mech hanger and must not touch sprockets."
   - Model a hanger on the dropout, and the derailleur body hanging from it: parallelogram, cage, upper and lower pulley.
   - Add the visible screws (L, H, B) and the barrel adjuster where the bike has one.
   - Add a test in `stage.test.ts` that the derailleur and the chain clear the sprockets in every gear, like the existing clearance tests.
2. **Bottom bracket.** "The bottom bracket looks very low. Increase the height." Raise it to a typical height for each bike type (`BBY` and the frame points in `bikeSpec`), and keep the chain, the clearance tests and the views right.
3. **Shifters and handlebar.** "The shifter needs more detail, also depending on road bike lever vs regular shifting. The handlebar should also change between flatbar and dropdown. … For a regular shifter you see both levers for shifting down / up."
   - Use `bike.handlebar` when it is known, else the bike type as today.
   - Drop bar: a combined brake and shift lever.
   - Flat or riser bar: a trigger shifter with both levers visible, next to the brake lever.
   - Check that the drop bar actually shows on road bikes (the owner did not notice it).
4. **Closer derailleur views.**
   - "The 'look at the rear derailleur' check should zoom in more on the derailleur." Adjust the pose of the view that `derailleur-alignment` uses.
   - Also fix `derailleur_from_behind` (listed under "Known 3D shortcomings" in the README). On a phone, the user must be able to judge whether the pulley is in line with the sprocket, also with the bike upside down.
5. **Preview for the garage.** Every bike in the garage gets its own 3D view, drawn from its facts. "You should be able to press individual parts of the bikes to see the generic name of that part ('derailleur') and if specified, additionally in small text the actual (short-version) part name ('Shimano XT 1x12')."
   - Add a preview mode to the stage: whole bike, slow rotation or drag to turn, tap a part for its tag.
   - Add a `model_fact` field to `NODES` (see the plan), and show that fact's value in small text when it is known.
   - Expose the mode through a small, documented API that A1 calls from the garage, for example `stage.preview(facts, element)`. Write the exact API in your handoff.
   - Don't build the garage screen itself; A1 does.
6. **Optional, last: fit the frame to the geometry facts.**
   - Map the `geometry.*` facts that change the silhouette (stack, reach, head and seat angle, chainstay, bottom bracket drop, wheel size) onto the frame points in `bikeSpec`.
   - Clamp to plausible ranges, and fall back to the defaults for any value that is missing or out of range.
   - Keep the clearance tests passing for extreme values.
   - Skip this if it would take more than about a third of the session, and say so in the handoff.
   - Cheap extra worth doing either way: the frame colour from `bike.colour` when it is present.
